# Section pattern catalog

The no-MCP fallback: common structural patterns for each landing-page section, as seen across live landing pages. Each line is **pattern** — what it looks like · what it optimizes for · what it gives up. Pick 3–6 patterns that differ in structure and priority, and that fit the product's real content (don't pick a 3-tier pattern for a one-plan product).

With the Landingfolio MCP connected, real screenshots replace this file: they show the level of polish too, which a pattern name can't.

**Contents:** Hero · Features · Pricing · Testimonials · Call to action · FAQ · Logo cloud · How it works · Stats · Footer · Header

## Hero (`hero`)

- **Centered statement** — headline, subhead, one or two CTAs centered; product shot full-width below · Clarity, works for any product · The product shows up late; generic if the visual is weak.
- **Split** — copy left, product visual right (often bleeding off the edge) · Shows the product in the first screen · Less room for a long headline; stacks to "copy then image" on mobile.
- **Product-first** — short headline over a large live UI, video or interactive demo that fills most of the fold · Proof by showing · Needs a genuinely good-looking product.
- **Proof-led** — rating/review count or customer logos above the headline, testimonial snippet next to the CTA · Trust for a skeptical audience · Busier; needs real proof.
- **Input hero** — the CTA is an input (email, URL, prompt) that starts the product right there · Time to value · Commits the visitor to one action.
- **Editorial** — oversized display type, little else, asymmetric layout · Memorable brand · Says less; weak for unknown products.
- **Visual-gallery** — a grid or marquee of outputs (templates, photos, designs) behind or under the headline · Shows range and quality of output · Can feel like a stock gallery if outputs are mediocre.

## Features (`feature`)

- **Three-up icon grid** — 3–6 cards with icon, title, one line · Scannable · Generic; every SaaS has one.
- **Alternating rows** — image left/text right, then swapped, one feature per row · Depth per feature · Long scroll.
- **Bento grid** — mixed-size cards, the big ones with product visuals · Hierarchy between features, modern feel · Hard to make content fill every cell evenly.
- **Tabbed showcase** — tabs or a list on one side switch a large visual on the other · Many features in one screen · Hidden content; needs JS.
- **Sticky scroll** — copy steps scroll while a visual stays pinned · Narrative · Fragile on mobile.
- **Outcome-led** — each block is a result ("Ship in a day") with the feature as proof under it · Speaks benefits · Needs concrete outcomes.

## Pricing (`pricing`)

- **Three tiers, middle highlighted** — "Most popular" badge, monthly/annual toggle · Steers the choice · Pushes everyone to compare.
- **Single plan** — one card, one price, full feature list · Zero decision friction · No upsell.
- **Comparison table** — tiers as columns, features as rows with checks · Answers "what's the difference" · Dense; poor on mobile.
- **Usage slider / calculator** — price updates as the visitor sets seats or volume · Fair-feeling for usage pricing · Hides the headline number.
- **Proof-first pricing** — testimonials, logos or a guarantee directly above or beside the plans · Reduces risk at the decision point · Longer section.
- **Free vs paid** — two cards, free on the left, paid on the right with the delta spelled out · Clear upgrade story · Only fits freemium.

## Testimonials (`testimonial`)

- **Wall of love** — masonry grid of many short quotes with avatars · Volume of proof · Individual quotes get skimmed.
- **Single hero quote** — one large quote with photo, name, role, company logo · Weight of one credible voice · Relies on one customer.
- **Carousel** — one or three at a time with arrows/dots · Compact · Most visitors see only the first.
- **Case-study cards** — logo + metric ("−40% churn") + short quote + link · Concrete outcomes · Needs real numbers.
- **Video testimonials** — thumbnails with play buttons · Authentic · Heavy; few people press play.
- **Social embeds** — quotes styled as tweets/posts · Feels unsolicited · Can look informal.

## Call to action (`call-to-action`)

- **Full-width band** — contrasting background, headline, one button · Unmissable · Repetitive if every section ends that way.
- **Card CTA** — a contained card with visual on one side · Feels designed, not shouty · Easier to scroll past.
- **Repeat the hero** — mirror the hero's headline and input · Consistency for scanners who reach the end · Adds nothing new.
- **Objection-handling CTA** — button plus 2–3 risk reducers (free trial, no card, cancel anytime) · Converts hesitant visitors · More text near the button.

## FAQ (`faq`)

- **Accordion** — questions collapse, one column · Compact · Answers hidden from scanners and some crawlers.
- **Two-column** — heading/contact prompt left, accordion right · Uses wide screens · Stacks to a plain accordion on mobile.
- **Open grid** — all answers visible in a 2–3 column grid · Scannable, SEO-friendly · Long if answers are long.

## Logo cloud (`logo-cloud`)

- **Single muted row** — 5–8 greyscale logos under the hero · Quiet credibility · Easy to ignore.
- **Marquee** — scrolling strip · Fits many logos · Motion can distract.
- **Logos + metric** — "Trusted by 4,000 teams" with the row · Adds scale · Needs a true number.

## How it works (`how-it-works`)

- **Numbered steps** — 3 steps in a row, number, title, one line · Simplicity · Abstract without visuals.
- **Steps with visuals** — each step shows the UI at that step · Concrete · Needs good screenshots.
- **Before → after** — the problem state next to the result · Shows the transformation · Needs a clear visual contrast.

## Stats (`stats`)

- **Big-number row** — 3–4 large figures with labels · Instant scale · Meaningless if numbers are small or vague.
- **Stats + quote** — figures beside a customer quote · Pairs scale with a human · Busier.

## Footer (`footer`)

- **Link columns** — logo + tagline left, 3–5 link columns right · Navigation · Standard; low impact.
- **CTA footer** — a final CTA block merged into the footer · One last conversion chance · Must not compete with the section above.
- **Minimal** — one line of links and copyright · Clean · Loses SEO links and navigation.

## Header (`header`)

- **Logo left, links center, CTA right** · Familiar · Indistinct.
- **Floating pill** — a rounded bar detached from the top edge · Modern, light · Can cover content when sticky.
- **Announcement bar + nav** — a thin promo strip above the nav · Surfaces a launch or offer · Banner blindness.
